# Synth

Open `index.html`. Click the play icon or press Space. The slider beside it controls BPM. No dependencies or build step. The initial patch is captured in `INITIAL_STATE` in `synth.js`: 122 BPM, 1/16 rate, C add9, seven unison voices, linked eight-step lanes, and the saved desktop card positions. Playback starts stopped.

- **Chords:** major chords on the outer ring, minor chords on the inner ring. The selectors choose extension and inversion. Click the chord name to audition it. To capture a four-bar progression, click the circle button beneath the wheel and select four chords. Each click leaves a numbered wheel marker and fills one slot. The fourth click starts the loop automatically, with one chord per 4/4 bar. Extension and inversion are captured with each selection; repeated chords are allowed. The loop arrow enables/disables the saved progression, and × clears it. Clicking the capture button again cancels an unfinished capture. Captured progressions are kept for the current page session.
- **Arp:** note, gate, velocity and octave are tabs over one graph. Draw values in the graph; the small buttons below the note view mute individual steps. The playhead column and active marker follow the selected lane's own clock. Each tab retains its own length and division. `len` changes the current lane's length; `÷` changes its clock division. The link icon synchronizes lengths. The ↺ toggle restarts all lanes from step one on each chord selection or voicing change; off, their phases keep running. Randomize affects the selected lane. The power icon bypasses the arp and holds the full chord. With a chord progression enabled, bypass plays each complete chord for its bar. Retrigger resets the arp lanes at each chord/bar; off, they keep their independent phases across bar boundaries.
- **Synth:** waveform buttons select the oscillator shape. Position blends toward sine. The voice-line display has two vertical sliders: voices on the left, continuous detune on the right. The central graph is passive. Shift-drag a slider for fine control; double-click either slider to restore its captured default. Both support arrow keys and Home/End; detune uses 0.1-cent arrow steps and 0.01-cent Shift-arrow steps. A sine sub and a resonant low-pass filter complete the voice path. Drag ADSR points or use the A/D/S/R sliders. Knob values appear on hover or focus. Drag knobs vertically, hold Shift for fine adjustment, or double-click to reset. Waveform, position, detune, cutoff and resonance update sounding notes; unison count, sub level and ADSR settings apply to new notes.
- **Keyboard:** A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play chromatic notes. Z/X shift octave. Notes sustain until released. Focused controls retain their own keyboard behavior. Tabs support Left/Right and Home/End; graph values support arrow keys and Home/End, with Enter/Space to mute.
- **Layout:** drag empty panel padding on desktop. Smaller screens stack and scroll. The small dotted-circle button toggles animation. The system reduced-motion preference is respected. Disabling animation freezes plant poses and clears rain and fireworks. Playback continues when switching tabs, windows or desktops. Held computer-keyboard notes release on focus loss so missed key-up events cannot leave them stuck.

Unison controls petal count, detune controls spread, position controls curvature, sub controls stem height, ADSR shapes blooms, cutoff opens leaves, resonance adds ripples, and output level controls brightness. Rain occupies a separate full-screen layer and falls diagonally across the entire viewport. Smoothed note activity and envelope energy increase its density and speed, with a light drizzle at rest. The garden uses projected 3D petals, cupped leaves and a perspective ground plane. Daisies, cosmos, poppies, hanging bells and seedheads mix with ferns, reeds and leafy sprigs. The denser meadow has 76 plants across foreground and distant flower patches, plus up to 900 grass tufts (fewer on smaller screens). Each plant has its own silhouette, tilt, size, depth and wind rhythm, randomized once per page load and preserved when resizing. Critically damped motion keeps a pronounced, quicker sway smooth rather than sluggish. Blooms are larger, and the petal count follows voice-count changes with a much faster response. No note directly jumps a plant pose. Petals, leaves and bell flowers have opaque black silhouettes. Grass and plants are interleaved by depth, and the weather layers sit behind the garden, so background lines cannot show through petals. Quieter distant lines retain the perspective. Much larger randomized fireworks burst on every 4/4 downbeat while playback runs, including repeated or unchanged chords. They follow the audio/BPM bar clock independently of the arp rate and remain behind the cards. Pitch still adds accents to individual plants.

The scope reads actual audio output. The oscillator graph shows the selected waveform blend, the filter graph shows its frequency response, and the envelope shows ADSR settings. The compact tabbed arp and its independent lane playheads follow the local ArpStick research and Caligo's arp UI.

## Unison tuning

Detune is a continuous fractional-cent offset around the played note, limited here to ±50 cents. A quadratic control curve gives extra precision at the low end; the visual follows that control curve. The display, note-on tuning and live retuning use the same symmetric voice positions. An odd voice count includes the original pitch; one voice stays at the original pitch for any detune setting. Live changes are smoothed with a 12 ms time constant. No octave/fifth stacks are applied by unison. The arp's note-degree wrapping and octave lane still transpose notes independently, as saved in the default patch.

References checked: [Serum 2's official guide, pp. 45–48](https://xferrecords.com/manual/serum-2/docs), [Surge XT's oscillator reference](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/manual-xt/#oscillator-algorithms), and Vital's [parameter declarations](https://github.com/mtytel/vital/blob/main/src/common/synth_parameters.cpp), [voice tuning](https://github.com/mtytel/vital/blob/main/src/synthesis/producers/synth_oscillator.cpp) and [unison display](https://github.com/mtytel/vital/blob/main/src/interface/editor_sections/oscillator_section.cpp). This implementation uses a deliberately narrow range for gentle thickening rather than reproducing every plugin mode.

The procedural scene lives in `garden.js`, independently of the audio engine. It renders on each animation frame, with stable spring integration and a capped frame delta after interruptions. Rain density and active firework counts remain bounded.
